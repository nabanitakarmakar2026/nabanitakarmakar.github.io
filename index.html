01 / ABOUT

About me

I am a researcher in applied mathematics whose work focuses on mathematical modelling of water-wave interaction with coastal and offshore structures. My doctoral research investigates wave scattering and the mitigation of hydrodynamic forces acting on submerged tunnels using porous breakwaters, thin porous barriers, and different types of seabed topography.

My approach combines analytical formulation, mathematical analysis, and numerical computation. I am interested in extending this work to more complex marine problems, including nonlinear wave effects, hydroelasticity of floating structures, and renewable offshore energy systems.
